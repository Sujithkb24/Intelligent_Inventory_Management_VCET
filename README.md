# EV Smart Inventory Intelligence

### AI-Powered Inventory Optimization and Predictive Procurement for the EV & Automotive Industry

## Overview

The electric vehicle (EV) and automotive manufacturing industry relies on complex supply chains involving critical components such as battery cells, Battery Management Systems (BMS), motor controllers, automotive microcontrollers, and onboard chargers. Delays in sourcing these components, unexpected supplier disruptions, and inefficient inventory allocation can significantly affect production schedules and customer delivery commitments.

**EV Smart Inventory Intelligence** is an intelligent inventory management and predictive procurement solution designed to address these challenges through real-time inventory visibility, predictive analytics, intelligent order allocation, and AI-powered decision support.

The system continuously monitors material availability, evaluates customer order priorities, identifies potential shortages, and predicts delivery risks. By combining Machine Learning with an AI procurement agent, it helps manufacturers move from reactive inventory management towards proactive and data-driven supply chain decisions.

## Problem Statement

EV manufacturers face several critical inventory and supply chain challenges:

- **Component Shortages:** Critical components such as battery cells and automotive chips are vulnerable to supply shortages.
- **Supplier Uncertainty:** Long and unpredictable supplier lead times can disrupt production schedules.
- **Production Delays:** Material shortages can prevent manufacturers from fulfilling customer orders within committed delivery dates.
- **Inefficient Inventory Allocation:** Limited inventory may not be distributed effectively across orders with different delivery priorities.
- **Reactive Procurement:** Conventional inventory systems often identify shortages only after they begin affecting production.
- **Limited Supply Chain Visibility:** Manufacturers lack a unified view of inventory, incoming materials, supplier reliability, and order risks.

## Our Solution

We propose an intelligent inventory management platform that integrates inventory tracking, predictive risk assessment, automated material allocation, and AI-assisted procurement into a unified decision-support system.

The platform transforms inventory and supplier data into actionable insights, enabling manufacturers to anticipate disruptions, evaluate procurement alternatives, and make informed decisions before production is affected.

## Key Features

### 1. Intelligent Inventory Monitoring
- Provides visibility into available, incoming, and allocated materials.
- Tracks inventory movements and material consumption.
- Identifies critical stock shortages and inventory discrepancies.
- Supports multi-level Bill of Materials (BOM) tracking for EV manufacturing.

### 2. Smart Order Allocation
- Prioritizes customer orders based on Customer Request Date (CRD).
- Allocates available materials according to production requirements.
- Identifies material constraints affecting individual orders.
- Improves inventory utilization across competing production orders.

### 3. Predictive Shortage and Delivery Risk Analysis
- Identifies materials that may become unavailable.
- Predicts potential supplier delivery delays using Machine Learning.
- Estimates projected order shipment dates.
- Classifies orders into Low, Medium, and High delivery-risk categories.

### 4. AI-Powered Procurement Intelligence
- Investigates the root cause of material shortages.
- Evaluates supplier reliability, lead times, and procurement costs.
- Identifies alternative suppliers and compatible materials.
- Generates intelligent purchase order recommendations with supporting evidence.

### 5. What-If Scenario Simulation
- Simulates unexpected supplier delays.
- Evaluates sudden changes in customer demand.
- Analyses the impact of additional material receipts.
- Compares order delivery risks before and after simulated disruptions.

### 6. Human-in-the-Loop Approval
- Generates purchase orders as drafts for review.
- Allows authorized procurement personnel to approve or reject recommendations.
- Maintains decision traceability and accountability.
- Ensures that procurement decisions remain under human control.

## How It Works

The platform follows an intelligent decision-making workflow:

1. **Data Collection:** Collects inventory transactions, customer orders, supplier information, and material availability data.
2. **Inventory Analysis:** Evaluates available stock, incoming materials, and production requirements.
3. **Order Prioritization:** Allocates materials based on customer delivery commitments.
4. **Risk Prediction:** Identifies shortages, supplier delays, and orders at risk of missing their delivery dates.
5. **AI Investigation:** Investigates bottleneck materials and evaluates possible procurement solutions.
6. **Recommendation Generation:** Creates purchase order recommendations based on supplier reliability, lead time, and material requirements.
7. **Human Approval:** Presents recommendations to authorized personnel for final review and approval.

## Project Limitations

The initial implementation is designed as a hackathon MVP. Its evaluation uses simulated inventory, supplier, and order data. Production-line constraints and manufacturing calendars are simplified, and actual supplier delivery commitments require human verification.
